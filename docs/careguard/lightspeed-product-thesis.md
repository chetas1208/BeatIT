# CareGuard — Product Thesis

## Wedge
Cardiology care-plan safety and evidence review — the cross-condition conflicts
missed when care is reviewed one organ at a time.

## Users
Cardiologists · advanced-practice clinicians · clinical pharmacists · care coordinators
· utilization-management teams.

## Buyers
Health systems · cardiology groups · clinical workflow platforms · EHR-integrated
software vendors.

## Measured workflow value (instrumented, not claimed)
review time · FHIR resources processed · relevant patient factors detected ·
contraindications detected · missing evidence detected · unsupported claims blocked ·
guideline citations verified · clinician accept/reject/override rate · model & tool
latency · cost per review. (See `evals.md` / success metrics.)

We do **not** claim improved patient outcomes, mortality reduction, diagnostic
accuracy, clinical efficacy, or money saved unless supported by real validation data.

## Why it wins
Auditable by construction: every value is sourced, math is deterministic, limits are
stated, and the clinician remains the decision-maker. That is exactly what makes a
clinical AI tool adoptable where black-box tools are not.

## Expansion
cardiometabolic review · cardiorenal review · oncology cardiotoxicity review ·
perioperative cardiac review · multispecialty guideline-conflict resolution ·
enterprise SMART-on-FHIR integration.
