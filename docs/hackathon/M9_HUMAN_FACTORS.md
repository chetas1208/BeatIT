# M9 Human Factors

M9 keeps one question visible at a time: what is the selected state, what
hypothetical change was made, what paired result was produced, and what
evidence supports or limits it. Observed state must remain visually distinct
from derived, simulated, prior, and synthetic material.

Acceptance checks:

- TWIN is the default landing space and shows the heart before secondary
  telemetry.
- EXPERIMENT labels every branch as hypothetical before controls or results.
- COMPARE names baseline and counterfactual on both sides and never implies a
  clinical intervention.
- EVIDENCE exposes why a value is unavailable and what provenance is retained.
- REPORT includes limitations and the canonical educational disclaimer.
- Loading, empty, and error states explain the next safe action and never use a
  fake progress percentage.
- Returning to TWIN does not silently replace an observed snapshot with a
  simulated one.
