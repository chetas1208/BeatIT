# BeatIT Pitch

## One line

BeatIT helps cardiologists explain and explore bounded recovery scenarios using
patient data and deterministic cardiovascular modeling—without allowing an LLM
to invent clinical calculations.

## Problem

Recovery planning after a cardiac event is difficult to personalize and explain
because patient evidence, physiological models, uncertainty, and care-team
reasoning remain disconnected.

## Insight

A cardiologist needs to show what is measured, what is inferred, what is
simulated, and why a bounded scenario changed—without presenting a hypothetical
model output as a patient prediction.

## Product

BeatIT turns supplied evidence into a reproducible baseline state, lets the
clinician run a bounded recovery scenario, compares the result visually, and
explains assumptions, provenance, uncertainty, and limitations.

## Technical novelty

The product separates language from numerical authority. AI structures evidence,
operates the workflow, and explains results; deterministic tested code performs
the cardiovascular calculations.

## User and business path

The initial user is a cardiologist in a supervised follow-up or education
workflow. Likely early buyers are cardiac rehabilitation programs, cardiology
service lines, and clinical education organizations. Start with a governed
education/workflow pilot and measure explanation burden, preparation time, and
comprehension—not patient outcomes.

## Research grounding and limits

The credibility package documents deterministic boundaries, expected-versus-
produced golden outputs, repeatability, uncertainty language, evidence mapping,
safety tests, and limitations. BeatIT is not clinically or regulatorily
validated and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, triage, or medical advice.
