# BeatIT Pitch

## Problem

Medical records describe events, but they do not make the assumptions,
uncertainty, and hypothetical consequences easy to inspect.

## Insight

An auditable computational twin is more useful than an opaque answer: preserve
the observed state, explore bounded alternatives, compare the same plausible
inputs, and expose what evidence is missing.

## Product

BeatIT is a multi-agent cardiac digital twin with a semantic beating heart,
timeline, deterministic physiology, plausible twins, Shadow Trial, Split Heart,
Missing Piece evidence analysis, provenance, and a report.

## Technical novelty

The product separates orchestration from numerical authority and keeps every
hypothetical result labeled, paired, reproducible, and inspectable.

## Research grounding and limits

The credibility package documents deterministic boundaries, uncertainty method,
evidence mapping, verification, and limitations. BeatIT is not clinically or
regulatorily validated and does not give medical advice.
